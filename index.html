The licensing route

New York City hires school secretaries through four routes. Here is where I sit, stated plainly.

I qualify now for the per diem substitute secretary certificate (PDSY). It requires a high school diploma or equivalent, the TSN application, fingerprints, and a principal nomination in Galaxy against a budgeted vacancy. There is no degree requirement and no experience requirement for that certificate. What is left is a nomination and the processing that follows it, and I am ready to start as soon as a principal nominates me.

I am also asking principals to consider me for the regular substitute route (5BA). That route asks for three years of approved office experience to enter, then 30 semester hours of college coursework within three years of hire. I already have 36 college credits, so the coursework half is finished before I start. What I do not have is a job title that says clerical. What I have is the work: enrollment and attendance records across several school sites, compliance logs that had to be right for state review, and weekly reconciliation of transaction records at a trading firm. I would rather a principal read that record than filter it out on a title.

One limit I will not hide: experience as a paraprofessional or a school aide does not count toward the secretary experience requirement, so I am not claiming that it does. The substitute certificate is the route open to me today, and it is the one I am asking to be nominated for.

What a nomination needs

  1. A principal nomination in Galaxy against a clear, budgeted vacancy in the school's Table of Organization.
  2. My TSN application, transcripts, and fingerprints.
  3. DHR processing after the nomination, which is when the certificate is issued.
  4. No application fee at any step.

End of day handover

Sample checklist
  • Cash and receipts counted, logged, and locked.
  • Phone messages logged with the time, the caller, and a number that was read back.
  • Absence calls entered before 10:00 AM, with callbacks noted.
  • Visitor badges returned and the sign in sheet closed.
  • Deliveries logged and placed in the staff mailbox.
  • Anything still open written on the handover note with a name and a time, not left in someone's memory.
